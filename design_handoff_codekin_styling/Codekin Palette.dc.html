Lightness is held constant across every option below, so the only thing moving is saturation and temperature.

The transcript is unchanged in all four — it is the warm scope and it stays warm. That contrast is the idea worth protecting; these options only reduce how loudly the cool side states it.

What changing this actually touches

  • Only the chrome neutrals: page, surface, surface-raised, edge, edge-strong. Sidebar text is already chroma 0, so nothing in the type changes.
  • The selected-row tint needs the same treatment or it will look bluer than everything around it. It carries slightly more chroma than the surfaces by design — keep the ratio, not the absolute value.
  • The intent families are untouched. Green “running”, amber “waiting”, terracotta error and the gold accent all stay as they are.
  • Light mode has its own chroma value and will need the equivalent reduction; verify separately rather than assuming it follows.
  • Check the status dots against the new surface at each step — the lower the surface chroma, the more the coloured dots stand out, which is generally an improvement.

I would take 5b. It is a single value, it keeps the hue relationship the design was built on, and it removes most of what reads as blue. If it still feels too cool after living with it, 5d is the next stop and it is the same one-line edit.
